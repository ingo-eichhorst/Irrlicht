## Approaches

- **baseline** — the pre-#753 model: pure observed round-rate, no estimate
  until the first round completes (the control).
- **prior-bootstrap** — show `total_rounds × prior` at zero rounds, then the
  pure observed rate once a round lands (identical to baseline thereafter).
  Shipped by #753; superseded by `production` below.
- **prior-blend** — Bayesian shrinkage toward the prior at every turn,
  `(w·prior + n·observed)/(w+n)`.
- **production** — the SHIPPED seam itself, `session.ForecastTaskCompletion`
  called directly (not reimplemented), so this row cannot drift from what
  users see. Currently prior-blend at `w=2.0` over a `4.0m` prior (#977). It
  tracks the `prior-blend` row exactly when that prior matches the corpus
  median; a gap between the two rows IS the drift called out under Corpus.
- **ewma** — exponentially-weighted per-round duration over the marker
  history (stateful; shippable only by widening the forecast seam).
- **token-aware** — considered and rejected: with no token *budget* target,
  remaining-time-from-tokens reduces algebraically to the elapsed round-rate
  (`remainingTokens × secsPerToken = (elapsed/completed) × remainingRounds`),
  so it adds no signal beyond rounds for *completion-time* prediction.

## Last mile — work after `completed == total` (#977)

The accuracy table above is blind to this by construction: its ground truth
is the episode's last marker, so every turn at `completed == total` has an
actual-remaining of 0 and is skipped. Measured here instead by following the
transcript past that marker.

How much work "follows the last marker" depends entirely on how long a pause
still counts as the agent working, so that cutoff is swept rather than
assumed. `has tail` is the fraction of completed episodes with ANY activity
after the final marker. The last two columns compare the two candidate
predictions at that moment: keeping the hard zero, or padding the forecast
with a wrap-up allowance scaled to the episode's own round rate.

| idle cutoff | episodes | has tail | median | p90 | max | median rounds | err @0 | err @0.40 rounds |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| 3.0m | 23 | 17% | 0s | 2.6m | 6.7m | 0.00 | 0s | 2.1m |
| 10.0m | 23 | 39% | 0s | 34.8m | 102.5m | 0.00 | 0s | 2.1m |
| 60.0m | 23 | 52% | 2.3m | 50.7m | 102.5m | 0.41 | 2.3m | 7.4m |

**The padding was rejected.** `err @0` wins at every cutoff, because the tail
is bimodal rather than centred: most completed episodes stop dead, and the
minority that keep going run far longer than any constant would cover. Zero
is the median-optimal prediction, so a blanket floor buys the minority a
little accuracy by making the majority worse. Note also how much of the
apparent tail is the cutoff's doing — a generous cutoff mostly measures a
human's absence, which is why #977's own ad-hoc figures (58% of episodes,
median 3.1m) resemble the widest row here rather than the narrow ones.

What remains wrong at the finish line is presentation, not arithmetic: the
UIs render "wrapping up" instead of a confident `<1m left` once every round
is reported done — true whether the tail is 0s or 40 minutes.

## Recommendation

Ship **production** — prior-blend at `w=2.0` over a 4.0m prior (#977).

- **Converges instead of anchoring (#977 failure mode #2):** shrinking the observed rate toward the prior in proportion to how few rounds back it cuts MAE from 241.6m (prior-bootstrap) to 195.5m, median |err| from 25.9m to 21.7m, and median relative error from 100% to 96%. A single unrepresentative first round now nudges the forecast rather than setting it — which is the issue's "increasingly accurate" requirement.
- **Still surfaces immediately (#753's property, preserved):** a number appears in 0s at 100% episode coverage — the zero-round path is the bare prior exactly as before.
- **Steadier:** jitter 2.4m → 59s between consecutive turns.
- **Rejected — the wrap-up floor (#977 failure mode #1).** Only 17% of completed episodes kept working past their final marker (median 0s), and predicting zero there is wrong by 0s at the median versus 2.1m for a 0.40-round pad. See the last-mile section: the tail is bimodal, so no constant fits it. Fixed in the UIs instead, by labelling that moment "wrapping up" rather than `<1m left`.
- **Watch the bias.** Blending trades a worse mean signed error (-3458s → -7999s) for the better MAE/median above; the corpus is dominated by a few very long episodes, so the median columns are the more robust read.
- Lowest-MAE swept blend was 189.8m (median 20.8m); the shipped weight trades a little mean for the better median rather than chasing MAE alone.
- The zero-round number is still shown with a deliberately wide (≈2×) range to signal a population prior, not a measured rate.
